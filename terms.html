Purpose

HajriBanao is a local employee attendance and salary recordkeeping tool. You are responsible for entering accurate information, for keeping backups and for following your local employment and privacy laws.

Salary calculations

Salary figures use the app's configurable working-days formula, not government payroll/tax rules. The app is not legal, accounting or tax advice, and does not transfer any money.

Privacy and employee rights

Only enter employee personal information that you are authorized to maintain. Protect devices and exported backups, and provide appropriate workplace notices.

Availability and data loss

Data is stored on your current device in browser/WebView storage. Clearing app data, browser data or uninstalling can permanently erase records. Use backups regularly.
