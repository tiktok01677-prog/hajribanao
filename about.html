About HajriBanao

A simple attendance and payroll ledger for small teams.

Why HajriBanao?

HajriBanao helps shopkeepers, offices and small business owners manage employee records without paper attendance sheets. The interface adapts to mobile screens, and includes English/Urdu options, dark mode, exports and local backups.

What it does

Employee management, daily Present/Absent/Paid Leave marking, attendance summaries, monthly salary estimates, advance/payment records, CSV reports, printable salary slips and JSON backup/restore.

What it does not do

No automatic cloud synchronization, payroll transfer, bank transactions, government filings, tax/legal advice or guaranteed recovery of records. Salary calculations are based on the rule shown in the app.
