Privacy Policy

How HajriBanao handles employee, attendance, and payroll records.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

HajriBanao is a local-first employee attendance and salary bookkeeping tool. This statement describes the provided website source. Review it before distributing a modified Android WebView app.

Information entered into the app

You may choose to enter employee names, job roles, phone numbers, joining dates, active status, monthly salary, daily attendance (Present, Absent and Paid Leave), salary payments, advances, payment dates and notes, as well as your business name and preferences.

What the information is used for

These records are used locally to show employee lists, attendance totals, salary estimates, balances, histories and printable salary slips. The provided version does not perform bank transfers or government payroll filings.

Storage and sharing

In the supplied website, records are stored in browser localStorage (or Android WebView storage) on your own device. No developer-controlled database, account sign-in, automatic cloud sync, analytics, ads, or third-party data-sharing SDK is included in the provided source.

Website hosting

When you open a hosted copy of HajriBanao, the hosting provider, such as GitHub Pages, may collect IP addresses and other technical connection logs under its own privacy terms. This is separate from the employee ledger stored on your device.

Exports and backups

When you export CSV records or download a JSON backup, that file contains your employee and payment information in readable form. It is your responsibility to store, share and remove those files safely. Exports are not uploaded automatically by the app.

Data security

Local records are not encrypted with an app-specific password. Anyone who can access the browser/WebView profile or your backup file may be able to read them. Protect your device, and obtain permission to handle employee personal information.

Retention and deletion

Records stay in local storage until you delete them in Settings, clear browser/WebView data, or remove the app's data. Previously downloaded backups and exports are not deleted automatically. Follow the data deletion instructions.

Children

The app is meant for businesses and employers, not children.

Changes

If you add online storage, login, ads or analytics later, you must review and update this policy and your Google Play Data Safety disclosures.
