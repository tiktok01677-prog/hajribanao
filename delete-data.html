Delete Your Data

How to erase HajriBanao employee records.

Delete all records inside the app

  1. Open HajriBanao on the device where the records are saved.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete all data and confirm the warning.
  4. Employee, attendance and salary records on that device will be deleted.

Delete individual employees or payments

Go to Employees and use the trash control. Deleting an employee also deletes their attendance and payment history. In Salary & Advances, individual recorded payments can also be deleted.

Clear website storage

You can also clear site data for the hosted HajriBanao website in your browser settings. For Android WebView, clear app storage from Android settings (this deletes all saved in-app records).

Downloaded files

CSV reports and JSON backups saved to Files/Downloads must be deleted separately. Clearing local site data cannot erase a file you previously exported.

Can deleted data be restored?

Only if you previously created and kept a JSON backup. There is no automatic cloud recovery in this version.
